## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm e621 reading room where the art is the only bright thing. Each post is a navy e621 panel with the artist line on top, the picture inset, and favourite, score and tags under it. It refuses the category default of edge-to-edge photos with overlaid hearts and a centred creator ribbon.

OWN-WORLD: e621 Hexagon navies (#020F23 ground, #152F56 panels, #1F3C67 raised), link blue #B4C7D9, and one gold (#E8C446) reserved for the main action, "New" and the current tab. e621 tag category colours on tags. Rounded system face for titles, names, numbers and buttons. Pressable controls carry a 2–3 px darker same-hue lip that sinks on press. Kaleidoscope avatars. A floating glass capsule tab bar.

STORY: Open Following and see "12 new since your last visit". Catch up quickly, fave with one thumb, reach "You're caught up", find an artist to follow, and press the round gold ▶ to play it all lean-back.
