Direction A v4: e621 colours, iOS structure

Same e621 navies, gold and tag colours as v3. The structure now follows iOS conventions: large titles, a floating glass tab bar and pill buttons. Type is the iPhone's own system font. Spacing and component rules come from a study of AllTrails (docs/path-a/research/alltrails-study.md), applied to our own content rather than copied.

Placeholder art only.

What changed from v3

  • Bottom bar. It now floats, the way iOS does it: a navy glass capsule above the content, icons with labels, and the selected tab in a lighter pill with a gold icon. The Play pill from the UX plan floats just above it.
  • Posts. The picture comes first, rounded, with nothing on top of it. Underneath are three lines in a fixed order: who made it, e621's stats (with the favourite button), then the coloured tags. There's no box around a post. The space does the grouping.
  • Suggestions. Artist cards you swipe sideways, one artist per card: one large and two small recent posts, then the name and Follow.
  • Artist page. Big name, the three numbers that matter in a plain row, then one primary gold pill (Play all) and one secondary pill (Following).
  • Type. The system font (SF Pro on iPhone) in iOS's own sizes, instead of Verdana.
