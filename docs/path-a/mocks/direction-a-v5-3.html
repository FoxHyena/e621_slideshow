Direction A v5.3: e621 colours, iOS structure, a playful touch

v4 plus three things. (1) The fixes from the first design review. (2) Lessons from the Duolingo study, applied our way: buttons you press have a solid edge that sinks when tapped, titles and numbers use a rounded face, and each stat has its own colour. (3) The rest of the key screens: player, Settings and sign-in.

Gold now means one thing: the main action on each screen (Play, Play all, Review, Sign in) plus where you are (the selected tab and new posts). Placeholder art only.

What changed from v4

  • Gold is used once per screen. Follow buttons are navy with a gold plus. A selected filter gets a gold ring instead of a gold fill. Artist names are white.
  • Posts sit in navy panels, like e621's own post boxes. The artist line is at the top of the panel (owner, v5.1 sign-off), then the picture, then favourite, score and tags 12 below it. Everything inside the panel sits on one 12 px inner edge (v5.3). 24 between posts. "New" is the only gold on a post.
  • Tags are buttons. One row you can swipe, each tag 32 tall with a 44 touch area. The artist tag is gone, since it repeated the name.
  • Play is a round button, bottom right (owner, v5.1 sign-off), in thumb reach. A small "12" on it says how many new posts it plays. It fades while you scroll down and comes back when you stop or scroll up.
  • Pressable things feel pressable. Main buttons have a 3 px solid edge (2 px on small ones) in a darker shade of their own colour. It closes in 100 ms when pressed.
  • Rounded voice. Titles, names, numbers and buttons use the iPhone's rounded system face (Nunito where that isn't available). Reading text stays on the regular face.
  • A small celebration. "You're caught up" gets the kaleidoscope mark and a short line. It's shown once per visit, and never on top of art.
