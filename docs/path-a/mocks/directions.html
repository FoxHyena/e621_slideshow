Pick a look for e621 Slideshow

The same Following screen three times, at real phone size (375 × 812, one CSS pixel per iPhone point). Same spacing, same content, same placeholder art. Only colour, type, shape and one signature detail change. Scroll inside each phone to see a burst, the caught-up divider and the one-artist suggestion card. Hearts, Follow, chips and Next artist respond to taps.

Placeholder art only. Avatars are the existing kaleidoscope style.

Spacing fixes from your notes (same in all three)

  • Avatar sat too far right. It now starts at the 16 px edge, the same line as the title, chips and tags. Name and time start at 64.
  • ⋯ felt uneven. It's now a real 44 px button whose dots end on the right 16 px edge, mirroring the avatar. The header icons do the same.
  • Artist tiles looked janky. The suggestion card is one surface. The 3 tiles sit edge to edge inside it with 2 px gaps, so there are no boxes inside boxes.
  • Turn on Show keylines to check: everything lines up on 16, 64 and 359.

My recommendation

B, Plush hang tag. It's the only option that hits all three of playful, cute and slightly premium. Its frame is less than half as colour-cast as the e621 navy, so the art keeps its own colours. The tag detail is limited to three places: the Follow button, selected chips and the caught-up divider. That keeps it from turning into a costume.

If you'd rather the app feel like a quiet gallery than a cute app, C is the one. A is the safe choice if you want it to feel like part of e621.
