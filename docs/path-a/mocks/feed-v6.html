Why v5.3 reads cheap, and what v6 does about it

In v5.3In v6
Every surface is the same flat navy with the same hairline: toolbar circles, chips, panels, the fav pill, tags. Nothing says which things matter.Three materials. Things you press are raised, lit from above, with a solid edge that sinks. Things you browse (the picture, tags) are sunk into the panel. Panels are lit navy. The hairlines are gone.
The picture is boxed twice with corners that don't nest (8 inside 16) and ends up 319 wide.One 8 edge inside the panel. Panel corners 24, picture corners 16, so they nest. The picture is 327 wide and sits in a dark rim.
The avatar is a 24 px pie chart. The one thing only this app has is the smallest thing on the card.A hashed mark at 36: two bent tubes around a dot, the tab bar's own shape, in the app's colours. Gold dot when the post is new. It spins once when you favourite.
Everything under the picture is 13 px, so the row of tags is the loudest thing after the art.Name 17, counts 15, tags 13, time and post number 13 under the name. Tags become a quiet caption under the picture; the favourite row anchors the bottom corner.
A toolbar row with three loose circles floats above the title. "12 new", the reason you opened the app, is a grey subtitle.Title and tools share one row, the tools in one capsule. Under it: the faces of who posted, and "12 new from 5 artists".
Play and the tab bar cut straight through whatever card is under them.The feed fades into the ground under the bottom stack. Play gets a deeper edge and a real shadow, since it floats.
Favouriting pops the heart and nothing else.White goo beads along the bottom edge of the heart button, sags, and the heaviest drop lets go, in about 0.8 s. Double tap throws a big heart over the picture. Either way the avatar spins once and the drop lands on the tab bar. Following an artist makes their mark throw six small dots in its own colours.
Cards scroll past as flat content.Cards tuck. A card's top grows from 95% and rises 16 px as it comes in above the tab bar; its end shrinks to 95% and dims as it leaves under the header. The picture never moves while it is fully on screen.
