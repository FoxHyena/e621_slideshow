Direction A, refined for the art

e621's navy and gold stay, but the navy moves off the art. Pictures now sit on a neutral near-black, so nothing tints them. Navy is used only for things that never frame a picture: the tab bar, tags, cards, sheets and toasts. Gold marks what's new, what's selected and the main action.

Every size on these three screens comes from one small set: 5 text sizes, 2 control heights, 1 row height, 2 icon sizes. The full list is under the phones.

Reading order on every screen

  • 1. Screen title. The only 28 px text. It says where you are.
  • 2. The art. Full width, nothing on top except video controls.
  • 3. Who made it. Avatar and name, always in the same 56 px row on the same lines.
  • 4. Section headers. 17 px. Only where the feed changes from posts to something else.
  • 5. Details and controls. 13 px meta, compact chips and tags, in quiet colours, so they never compete with the art.
