Direction A v3: e621's Hexagon look, refined

Built from e621's own theme values, read from the e621ng source: its background, panel and bar navies, its gold, its tag category colours and its Verdana type. Posts sit in navy panels like an e621 post page. Each panel shows the post number, score, favourites and rating the way e621 does, and the tags are coloured by category. The bottom bar uses text tabs with a gold marker, like e621's menu, instead of icons.

The sizes still come from one short list (under the phones). Placeholder art only.

What moved away from Instagram

  • Feed. Each post is an e621-style navy panel, not an edge-to-edge picture.
  • Below the art. e621's stats line instead of a big heart row: favourite button with count, score in green, rating letter, post number.
  • Tags. Coloured text by category, like e621, instead of grey pills.
  • Look. Three e621 navies, e621 gold, Verdana. No generic near-black.
  • Bottom bar. Text tabs with a gold marker and a count badge, like e621's menu. No icon row.
  • Play. A squared gold button at the bottom right, not a centred pill.
