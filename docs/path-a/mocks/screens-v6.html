Approved by youThree things you asked for, built from the parts the app already has. Tap a picture and it opens alone on black, where you can zoom and save it, with no slideshow. Every post gets a page of its own with a link you can share. A post that belongs to a comic can be read page by page from either place. Both choices are picked by you: a double tap zooms, and a link shows the picture whatever the rating. The pictures are stand-ins.

1. The picture viewer

Tap a picture in a feed. It opens alone, at the width of the screen. Tap once to hide the buttons, double tap to zoom in where you tapped and again to zoom out. Drag down to close.

2. A comic page in the viewer

The same viewer with one more capsule: the comic's name and the page you are on. Drag sideways to turn the page. The count at the top follows.

3. All the pages

The capsule opens the app's sheet with every page as a thumb. The page you are on wears the gold ring. "Open as a feed" shows the comic as posts, in page order.

4. The post page

Where a shared link lands. The post as it is in a feed, then its comic, its description and every tag, where it came from, and more from the artist. It scrolls.

5. Sharing a post

A post's ⋯ gets one new row, "Open post". "Share post" now sends a link to the post page in this app, from every feed.

Under the pointer (hover)

Approved by you. Rows: InsetA pass over every kind of control, started by your two pictures: the tools capsule, where the highlight touched the capsule's edge, and the artist list, where it was a square block. Three rules, by what a control is made of. A button with a lip rises 1. A raised thing without a lip goes one step lighter. A flat or sunk thing gets a faint wash that stays inside its container. Pictures never change. On a computer, move the pointer over anything on this page to feel it. Each strip also shows the hover state fixed in place, so it reads on a phone.
