Screens v6

The screens the feed mock never drew, built from the approved v6 parts: the same three materials, sizes, Fredoka voice, artist marks and tab bar. Every phone scrolls and its controls work. The three open choices are picked: artist stats in wells, score and favs under grid tiles, Settings icons in wells. The goo on the tab bars is live: the heart on the Player phone adds to it, and Wipe clean is on the Settings phone.

Filter menus, player sheets and tag targets

Everything is live: tap a row, a switch, a tag. A menu marks the chosen row with a raised capsule that slides to it (picked), the same move as the tab bar and the segmented control.

Filter menus

A chip opens a sheet. Tapping a row applies it and closes the sheet; only Custom needs Apply. Tap Random to see its Reshuffle.

Player › Quick settings

The same controls as Settings › Slideshow, sitting straight on the sheet. Opening it pauses the slideshow; closing resumes.

Player › Post info

Who, three stats in wells, why For You picked it, then every tag by kind. Tap a tag to pop its targets.
