Scroll-first e621 Slideshow

Four tabs (Following, For You, Artists, Settings). The slideshow becomes a player you open from any feed. These grey boxes show layout and flow only; colors, type and motion come in the design step.

Following: your home

  • "New" uses e621's own marker, so e621.net and this app agree. The app moves it only once you've scrolled past every new post with no filters on. e621 can't move it back, so ⋯ › Mark all as seen asks first.
  • Pull down at the top to refresh. Tap the Following tab to jump to the top.

Artists and artist pages

How Back works

Tabs replace, screens push

/following→/artists→/artist/fox_artist→?play=123
  • Back closes the player, then the artist page, then goes to Following, then leaves the app.
  • Switching tabs never piles up history. Back from any tab goes to Following.
  • A pasted or shared link has nothing to go back to, so ‹ Back and ✕ go to the screen's parent instead of leaving.
  • In the installed app, a swipe from the left edge goes back too.
